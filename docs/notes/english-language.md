# Notes: English Language A 4EA1, Paper 1

## Confirmed spec facts
* 4EA1/01, Non-fiction Texts and Transactional Writing: 2 h 15 min, 90 marks, 60% of the qualification. Section A reading 45 (Q1 2, Q2 4, Q3 5, Q4 12, Q5 22); Section B transactional writing 45 (AO4 27 + AO5 18), one of two tasks.
* Text Two is always from Part 1 of the Pearson Anthology (10 non-fiction texts) and is printed in the real exam's source booklet. A paired 01R paper exists for other timezones.

## Deviations and decisions for the lead
* **Anthology text not reprinted.** Q4/Q5 and Section A items name the anthology text and author only (copyright); the student uses their own copy. A licensed copy or extract-booklet feature would make full papers self-contained.
* **Section A is one combined record** (qtype reading-section, parts a-e) and **Section B one EITHER/OR record** (transactional-choice), because the full-paper assembler fills slots independently and would otherwise mix Q1-Q3 from different unseen passages. Real numbering (Q1-Q7) would need question grouping or shared-figure support in the assembler.
* **Manual line numbers** in passages (numbered: false, numbers typed into the text) because the site splits passages into paragraph blocks and restarts built-in numbering in each. A line-offset option in the site would allow using the built-in numbering.
* **Spec refs invented** (C1.A.AO1.1a, C1.A.AO2.T01, C1.B.F1 ...): the spec has no numbered content statements.
* Unseen passages are 650-800 words; real Text One extracts are often closer to 1000.
* Standalone single-question items (Q1, Q2, Q3, Q4, Q5, single writing tasks) are also in the bank for topic practice.

## Corpus gaps
* Source booklets are not present for every series, so anthology-text counts in the style guide come from titles in question papers only (A Passage to Africa was not found).
