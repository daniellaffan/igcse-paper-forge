### history.russia-support.0006  (8 marks)
Source A: From the diary of a factory owner in Moscow, 1912.
The agitators are at work again. Since the shooting at the Lena goldfields, my men talk of nothing but strikes. Yet I pay better than most, and the government has now introduced insurance for sickness and accidents. The workers do not seem grateful. I shall ask the governor to send more police.
Source B: From a leaflet produced by a revolutionary group and handed out in St Petersburg, 1913.
Comrades! While the factory owners grow fat, our children go hungry. We work eleven hours a day for wages that will not buy enough bread. The Tsar's Duma does nothing for us. The workers of Lena were shot like dogs. Join the strike! Only our own strength will change our lives.
 Study Sources A and B.
