# english-language — cold check batch english-language-012

Answer every question as a strong student would.

### english-language.w.speech.cars  (45 marks)
 ‘Cars should be banned from the centre of every town and city.’

Write the text for a speech to be given to a meeting of your local youth council in which you explain your views on this statement.

Your speech may include:
• the benefits of car-free town centres
• the difficulties for some people and businesses
• any other points you wish to make.

Your answer will be assessed on how accurately and appropriately you use vocabulary, spelling, punctuation and grammar. [45 mark(s)]
