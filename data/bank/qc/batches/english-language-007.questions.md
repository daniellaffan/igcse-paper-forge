# english-language — cold check batch english-language-007

Answer every question as a strong student would.

### english-language.wchoice.029  (45 marks)
 Answer ONE of the following tasks, (a) or (b).

**EITHER**

**(a)** ‘Keeping animals in zoos can no longer be justified.’

Write an article for an online magazine in which you give your views on this statement.

Your article may include:
• the arguments for and against zoos
• how people can learn about wildlife in other ways
• any other points you wish to make.

**OR**

**(b)** A travel website is collecting guides to towns and cities written by local people.

Write a guide to your town or city for first-time visitors.

Your guide may include:
• places to visit and things to do
• practical information such as transport and food
• any other points you wish to make.

Your answer will be assessed on how accurately and appropriately you use vocabulary, spelling, punctuation and grammar. [45 mark(s)]

### english-language.wchoice.051  (45 marks)
 Answer ONE of the following tasks, (a) or (b).

**EITHER**

**(a)** A recent article in your local newspaper claimed that ‘parents today are too strict with their teenage children.’

Write a letter to the editor of the local newspaper in which you respond to this claim.

Your letter may include:
• why parents set rules
• how much freedom teenagers should have
• any other points you wish to make.

**OR**

**(b)** Your school magazine is publishing a series of articles with the title ‘Village or City?’.

Write your article for the series.

Your article may include:
• the attractions of village life
• what big cities can offer
• any other points you wish to make.

Your answer will be assessed on how accurately and appropriately you use vocabulary, spelling, punctuation and grammar. [45 mark(s)]

### english-language.wchoice.059  (45 marks)
 Answer ONE of the following tasks, (a) or (b).

**EITHER**

**(a)** Your headteacher is considering stopping all foreign-language lessons after the age of fourteen.

Write a letter to your headteacher in which you give your views on this idea.

Your letter may include:
• how this idea would affect students and the school
• what you think should happen instead, or how the idea could be improved
• any other points you wish to make.

**OR**

**(b)** A campaign group is encouraging people to save water.

Write the text for a leaflet persuading households to use less water.

Your leaflet may include:
• why saving water matters
• practical ways to save water
• any other points you wish to make.

Your answer will be assessed on how accurately and appropriately you use vocabulary, spelling, punctuation and grammar. [45 mark(s)]

### english-language.wchoice.049  (45 marks)
 Answer ONE of the following tasks, (a) or (b).

**EITHER**

**(a)** A recent article in a magazine you read claimed that ‘exams are not a fair way to measure what students can do.’

Write a letter to the editor of the magazine you read in which you respond to this claim.

Your letter may include:
• the strengths and weaknesses of exams
• other ways of assessing students
• any other points you wish to make.

**OR**

**(b)** A local newspaper is reviewing markets and shopping areas in the region.

Write a review of a market or shopping area you know well.

Your review may include:
• the range of stalls or shops
• the atmosphere and what makes it special
• any other points you wish to make.

Your answer will be assessed on how accurately and appropriately you use vocabulary, spelling, punctuation and grammar. [45 mark(s)]
