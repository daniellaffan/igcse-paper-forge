### english-language.w.leaflet.festival
- () levels-marked: AO4: Communicate effectively and imaginatively, adapting form, tone and register for purpose and audience (27 marks)
    L0 0: No rewardable material.
    LLevel 1 1-5: Communication is basic and often unclear. Little awareness of the purpose of the task or of the reader. Little sense of the required form, tone or register.
    LLevel 2 6-11: Communicates in a broadly suitable way. Some grasp of purpose and of what the reader needs. Form, tone and register are handled in a straightforward way.
    LLevel 3 12-17: Communicates clearly. A clear sense of purpose and of what the reader expects. Form, tone and register are appropriate.
    LLevel 4 18-22: Communicates successfully. Purpose and the reader's needs are securely met. Form, tone and register are used effectively.
    LLevel 5 23-27: Communication is perceptive and subtle. The writing is sharply focused on purpose and on the reader. Form, tone and register are handled with sophistication.
    • Purpose: to write the text of a leaflet that informs and persuades.
    • Audience: local people and visitors. The leaflet should capture their attention and encourage action.
    • Form: the response should be organised as the text of a leaflet, using a title, headings, short sections, bullet points or slogans, and a clear call to action or contact details.
    • Responses may:
    • describe music, art, food and workshops
    • use vivid, persuasive language
    • include dates, prices and travel information
    • use a confident, accessible tone with imperatives, persuasive language and clear organisational features
