# biology — cold check batch biology-recheck-3

Answer every question as a strong student would.

### biology.environment-prac-reliab.0002  (4 marks, calculator)
A student ran a tape measure from a hedge across a field and placed a quadrat at regular intervals along it.
a Explain why the student should repeat the investigation. [2 mark(s)]
b Suggest one variable, other than the distance from the hedge, that could affect the percentage cover of the plant in each quadrat. Explain how it could be controlled. [2 mark(s)]

### biology.environment-prac-reliab.0001  (4 marks, calculator)
Students used quadrats to compare the population of daisies in two areas of the school grounds.
a Explain why the students should repeat the investigation. [2 mark(s)]
b Suggest one variable, other than the area sampled, that could affect the number of daisies per quadrat. Explain how it could be controlled. [2 mark(s)]

### biology.environment-prac-reliab.0003  (4 marks, calculator)
Students placed quadrats at random on two areas of a rocky shore and recorded the species in each quadrat.
a Explain why the students should repeat the investigation. [2 mark(s)]
b Suggest one variable, other than the area of the shore, that could affect the number of different species per quadrat. Explain how it could be controlled. [2 mark(s)]
