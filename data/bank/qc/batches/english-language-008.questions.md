# english-language — cold check batch english-language-008

Answer every question as a strong student would.

### english-language.w.guide.camping  (45 marks)
 An outdoor activities centre is preparing information for groups of young visitors.

Write a guide to planning and enjoying a first camping trip.

Your guide may include:
• what to pack and how to choose a site
• how to stay safe and respect the countryside
• any other points you wish to make.

Your answer will be assessed on how accurately and appropriately you use vocabulary, spelling, punctuation and grammar. [45 mark(s)]
