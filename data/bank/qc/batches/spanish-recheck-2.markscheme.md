### spanish.s-conversation.a3.0012
- () levels-marked: Communication (both conversations, out of 15)
    L13–15 13-15: Very good: answers confidently, needing repetition only rarely; information always relevant; often develops ideas and opinions; justifies and explains several answers.
    L10–12 10-12: Good: answers well, occasionally needing an alternative question; information almost always relevant; sometimes develops ideas; gives reasons for some answers.
    L7–9 7-9: Satisfactory: copes with most questions but often needs the alternative questions; gives most of the information asked for, with some irrelevance; simple opinions.
    L4–6 4-6: Weak: struggles with many questions but tries to answer; some simple relevant information.
    L1–3 1-3: Poor: often fails to understand and replies with great difficulty; one or two basic relevant details.
    L0 0: Nothing creditable.
    • C1 Q1 (present): Sleep habits, e.g. *Duermo ocho horas.*
    • C1 Q2 (present): Exercise/activities in the present, e.g. *Corro dos veces por semana.*
    • C1 Q3 (opinion + reason): Opinion + reason (concentration, energy, mood).
    • C1 Q4 (past): Past: illness/symptoms and what happened.
    • C1 Q5 (conditional): Conditional + reason.
    • C2 Q1 (present): Problems with detail.
    • C2 Q2 (present): Personal actions, e.g. *Reciclo el papel.*
    • C2 Q3 (opinion + reason): Preference + reason.
    • C2 Q4 (past): Past weather.
    • C2 Q5 (future): Future actions.
- () levels-marked: Quality of Language (both conversations, out of 15)
    L13–15 13-15: Very good: wide range of syllabus structures and vocabulary used accurately, slips mainly in complex language; very good pronunciation, fluency and intonation.
    L10–12 10-12: Good: a range of structures and vocabulary with some errors; good pronunciation and fluency despite some hesitation.
    L7–9 7-9: Satisfactory: some syllabus structures and adequate vocabulary with frequent errors; pronunciation and fluency acceptable despite errors and hesitation.
    L4–6 4-6: Weak: narrow range of structures and vocabulary, seldom accurate; often ambiguous; understood only with effort; stilted delivery.
    L1–3 1-3: Poor: very narrow range, nearly always inaccurate; pronunciation rarely comprehensible.
    L0 0: Nothing creditable.
    • Look for accurate verbs in past, present and future/conditional time frames.
    • Credit linked, extended sentences (porque, aunque, sin embargo, cuando, si …) and opinion phrases.
    • Credit topic vocabulary used accurately and pronunciation that is easily understood.
- general: Award one mark out of 15 for Communication and one out of 15 for Quality of Language across BOTH conversations together. Start at the bottom band and work up; top of band if the descriptor is convincingly met, bottom if just met.
