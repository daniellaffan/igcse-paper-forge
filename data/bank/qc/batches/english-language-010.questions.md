# english-language — cold check batch english-language-010

Answer every question as a strong student would.

### english-language.w.letter.shopping  (45 marks)
 A recent article in a magazine you read claimed that ‘we buy far too many things we do not need.’

Write a letter to the editor of the magazine you read in which you respond to this claim.

Your letter may include:
• why people buy more than they need
• the effects on people and the planet
• any other points you wish to make.

Your answer will be assessed on how accurately and appropriately you use vocabulary, spelling, punctuation and grammar. [45 mark(s)]

### english-language.w.letter.jobs  (45 marks)
 A recent article in your local newspaper claimed that ‘teenagers should have part-time jobs while they are still at school.’

Write a letter to the editor of the local newspaper in which you respond to this claim.

Your letter may include:
• the advantages of part-time work
• the risks for students' studies and well-being
• any other points you wish to make.

Your answer will be assessed on how accurately and appropriately you use vocabulary, spelling, punctuation and grammar. [45 mark(s)]

### english-language.w.letter.adventure  (45 marks)
 Your headteacher is considering cancelling all school trips and outdoor activities for safety reasons.

Write a letter to your headteacher in which you give your views on this idea.

Your letter may include:
• how this idea would affect students and the school
• what you think should happen instead, or how the idea could be improved
• any other points you wish to make.

Your answer will be assessed on how accurately and appropriately you use vocabulary, spelling, punctuation and grammar. [45 mark(s)]
