# english-language — cold check batch english-language-009

Answer every question as a strong student would.

### english-language.w.leaflet.festival  (45 marks)
 Your town is holding a new arts festival.

Write the text for a leaflet advertising the festival.

Your leaflet may include:
• the events and performers
• practical information for visitors
• any other points you wish to make.

Your answer will be assessed on how accurately and appropriately you use vocabulary, spelling, punctuation and grammar. [45 mark(s)]
