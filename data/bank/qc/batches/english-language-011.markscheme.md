### english-language.w.review.trip
- () levels-marked: AO4: Communicate effectively and imaginatively, adapting form, tone and register for purpose and audience (27 marks)
    L0 0: No rewardable material.
    LLevel 1 1-5: Communication is basic and often unclear. Little awareness of the purpose of the task or of the reader. Little sense of the required form, tone or register.
    LLevel 2 6-11: Communicates in a broadly suitable way. Some grasp of purpose and of what the reader needs. Form, tone and register are handled in a straightforward way.
    LLevel 3 12-17: Communicates clearly. A clear sense of purpose and of what the reader expects. Form, tone and register are appropriate.
    LLevel 4 18-22: Communicates successfully. Purpose and the reader's needs are securely met. Form, tone and register are used effectively.
    LLevel 5 23-27: Communication is perceptive and subtle. The writing is sharply focused on purpose and on the reader. Form, tone and register are handled with sophistication.
    • Purpose: to write a review that informs, describes and evaluates.
    • Audience: readers of the publication or website, who want an engaging, honest judgement.
    • Form: the response should be set out as a review, with a title, an introduction, description and evaluation, and a clear final judgement or recommendation.
    • Responses may:
    • describe the day in an engaging way
    • evaluate the educational value and enjoyment
    • suggest improvements for future trips
    • combine description with clear evaluative language, perhaps using a rating or recommendation
- () levels-marked: AO5: Write clearly, using a range of vocabulary and sentence structures, with appropriate paragraphing and accurate spelling, grammar and punctuation (18 marks)
    L0 0: No rewardable material.
    LLevel 1 1-3: Ideas are expressed with limited use of structural and grammatical features. Basic vocabulary, often misspelt. Basic control of punctuation; sentences are undeveloped and repetitive.
    LLevel 2 4-7: Ideas are expressed and ordered, with paragraphs and a range of structural and grammatical features. Regular spelling patterns are mostly correct. Some control of punctuation; a range of sentence types including coordination and subordination.
    LLevel 3 8-11: Ideas are developed and linked; paragraphing and grammatical features make meaning clear. Varied vocabulary; irregular spellings mostly correct. Accurate, varied punctuation; sentence structures adapted as needed.
    LLevel 4 12-15: Ideas are managed cohesively, with structural and grammatical features used deliberately across the whole text. Wide, well-selected vocabulary with only occasional spelling errors. Punctuation placed for clarity; sentences controlled for deliberate effect.
    LLevel 5 16-18: Complex ideas are handled with control, using a range of structural and grammatical features for coherence and cohesion.
