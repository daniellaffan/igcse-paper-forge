# english-language — mark scheme for batch english-language-001

### english-language.q2.whiteout.1
- () Accept any reasonable points in the candidate's own words, where possible, 1 mark per valid point, up to a maximum of 4 marks. For example:
• he sets off early in clear, sunny weather
• the snow on the mountain top is sparkling
• he has the right equipment, including a map, compass and spare gloves
• the forecast said it would stay dry
• he feels rather superior when he sees a group giving up
• he reaches the narrow ridge by late morning
• the ridge has steep slopes on both sides
• he enjoys wide views of lakes, farms and the sea
• he stops to eat and take photographs before noticing the cloud — 4  [Do not credit material taken from outside the lines given. Reward all valid points, including ones not listed here. Points should be expressed in the candidate's own words; do not credit long, unselective copying. Comment on language (AO2) is not required.]

### english-language.q2.cliffharvest.2
- () Accept any reasonable points in the candidate's own words, where possible, 1 mark per valid point, up to a maximum of 4 marks. For example:
• the ropes are tied to iron stakes that have been there for generations

• Eyrún hangs about sixty metres below the top, swinging against the rock
• she uses a long pole with a net to collect birds from the ledges
• huge numbers of adult birds circle and shriek around her
