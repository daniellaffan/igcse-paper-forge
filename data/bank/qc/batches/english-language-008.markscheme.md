### english-language.w.guide.camping

    LLevel 4 18-22: Communicates successfully. Purpose and the reader's needs are securely met. Form, tone and register are used effectively.
    LLevel 5 23-27: Communication is perceptive and subtle. The writing is sharply focused on purpose and on the reader. Form, tone and register are handled with sophistication.

    • Audience: young people planning a camping trip. The guide should be clear, helpful and engaging.
    • Form: the response should be organised as a guide, using a title, headings, subheadings, lists or numbered steps, with a clear introduction and conclusion.
    • Responses may:

    • explain safety, weather and leaving no trace
    • describe the pleasures of outdoor life
    • address the reader directly, using imperatives, practical examples and a helpful, reassuring tone
