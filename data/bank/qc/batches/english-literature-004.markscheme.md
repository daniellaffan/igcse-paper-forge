    • **AO3:** Endings could be contrasted: *Half-past Two* closes with the child's escape back into ordinary time, with a sense that the timeless moment is never quite forgotten, whereas *Hide and Seek* closes with the discovery that the other children have gone, leaving the child alone in the dark.
