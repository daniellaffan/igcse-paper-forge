### history.russia-support.0006
- () levels-marked: Cross-reference
    LLevel 1 1–2: A simple comparison based on surface details of the sources, with little support taken from them.
    LLevel 2 3–5: A developed comparison identifying agreement and/or difference, supported with detail from both sources. Maximum 4 marks unless both agreement and difference are considered.
    LLevel 3 6–8: An analytical comparison of agreement and difference, well supported from both sources, with a reasoned overall judgement on how far Source A supports Source B.
    • Agreement: both sources suggest that workers were discontented and some wanted to strike. Source A: the men talk of nothing but strikes. Source B: it calls workers to join the strike.
    • Difference: Source A suggests that some workers were treated reasonably well (he claims he pays better than most), whereas Source B suggests that workers' living and working conditions were very poor (children go hungry and wages do not buy enough bread).
    • Difference: Source A suggests that the government was trying to help workers (the government has introduced sickness and accident insurance), whereas Source B suggests that the authorities treated workers as a threat to be controlled (it says the Duma does nothing for workers and that the Lena miners were shot).
    • Overall, Source A gives only limited support to Source B: they share some ground but differ on several points.
