### english-literature.unseen-market-morning.0002  (20 marks)
Read the following poem.
Market Morning
Before the sun is properly up
the market is already shouting:
mangoes! onions! two for one!
a radio arguing with a radio,

a cart of oranges bullied
through a gap no cart could fit,
a woman fanning fish with newspaper
as if she were cooling a fever.

Steam from the tea stall
climbs into the light. Coins
change hands like secrets.
A boy runs past with bread on his head

balanced like a crown.
Nobody here is still for long.
Everyone is selling something:
spice, shoes, stories, sweat.

By noon it will be folded up
in tarpaulin and string,
but now the whole street breathes in
and shouts it out again.

### english-literature.unseen-moving-day.0001  (20 marks)
Read the following poem.
Moving Day
The van came at eight and swallowed
everything we owned in an hour:
the sofa, the beds, the boxes marked
KITCHEN and FRAGILE and MISC.

Then the rooms were only rooms.
Our voices bounced off the walls
as if the house no longer knew us.

On the doorframe in the hall,
pencil lines climbed in a ladder:
my brother at three, me at five,
both of us at ten, then nothing.

Mum stood there a long time
with her coat on, keys in her hand.
She touched the lines once, lightly,
the way you touch a sleeping child.

Then she locked the door behind us
and posted the keys through the letterbox,
and we heard them land
like a full stop.

### english-literature.unseen-night-bus.0002  (20 marks)
Read the following poem.
Night Bus
Upstairs, front seat, the city
slides beneath me like a river
full of lit fish:
takeaways, a launderette,
a pharmacy's green cross blinking
on and off, on and off.

A nurse beside the window
sleeps with her mouth a little open.
Two boys share one pair of earphones,
nodding to the same small song.
A man in a suit reads nothing
from a phone gone dark.

We do not speak. We are strangers
going separate ways together,
our faces floating on the glass
among the shop signs and the rain,
each of us a small light
the night is carrying home.
