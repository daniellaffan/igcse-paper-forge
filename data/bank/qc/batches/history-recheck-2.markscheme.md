### history.russia-investigation.0006
- (b) levels-marked: Cross-reference
    LLevel 1 1–2: A simple comparison based on surface details of the sources, with little support taken from them.
    LLevel 2 3–5: A developed comparison identifying agreement and/or difference, supported with detail from both sources. Maximum 4 marks unless both agreement and difference are considered.
    LLevel 3 6–8: An analytical comparison of agreement and difference, well supported from both sources, with a reasoned overall judgement on how far Source A supports Source B.
    • Agreement: both sources suggest that workers were angry and ready to protest. Source A: some men want to strike again. Source B: the men talk of nothing but strikes.
    • Difference: Source A suggests that workers' living and working conditions were very poor (twelve people sleep in one room and workers are fined), whereas Source B suggests that workers' conditions were improving (he claims he pays better than most).
    • Difference: Source A suggests that the government did more to control workers than to help them (the police keep lists of workers' names, so the authorities watch rather than help them), whereas Source B suggests that the government was trying to help workers (the government has introduced sickness and accident insurance).

    LLevel 2 5–8: A developed answer that uses the extract, the sources and some own knowledge to comment on the interpretation, but may be one-sided. A judgement is given but is only partly supported.
    LLevel 3 9–12: An analytical answer that considers evidence both for and against the interpretation from the sources and own knowledge. The judgement is linked to the evidence but is not fully sustained.
    LLevel 4 13–16: A sustained evaluation that weighs evidence from the sources and precise own knowledge against the interpretation. A clear, fully justified judgement is reached and maintained.

    • Challenge from Source A: it suggests that the government did more to control workers than to help them (the police keep lists of workers' names, so the authorities watch rather than help them).
