    • **AO2 – *Search For My Tongue*:** The speaker imagines having two tongues in one mouth, an extended metaphor for living between two languages.
    • **AO2 – *Search For My Tongue*:** The fear that the mother tongue will 'rot' and be spat out conveys anxiety about losing one's culture.
    • **AO2 – *Search For My Tongue*:** The Gujarati lines in the centre of the poem show the language physically on the page, so form becomes part of the meaning.
    • **AO2 – *Search For My Tongue*:** Plant imagery of roots, veins, buds and blossom presents the language as alive and able to regrow.
    • **AO2 – *Half-caste*:** The speaker takes the term literally and mocks it with absurd examples from art, weather and music.
