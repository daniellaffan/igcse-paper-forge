### economics.protection-data.0001  (20 marks, calculator)
Extract A
The government of Zentara has introduced a quota on imports of rice. Domestic producers say they cannot compete with cheaper imports, some of which are sold below their cost of production.
a Before the quota, Zentara imported 90,000 tonnes of rice a year. The quota limits imports to 56,000 tonnes of rice a year. Calculate, to two decimal places, the percentage change in imports of rice. Show your working. [2 mark(s)]
b With reference to Extract A, analyse the possible benefits of the quota for Zentara. [6 mark(s)]
c Evaluate whether the quota on imports of rice will benefit the economy of Zentara. Use Extract A and your own knowledge of economics to support your answer. [12 mark(s)]

### economics.protection-data.0017  (20 marks, calculator)
Extract A
The government of Lumera has placed a 26% tariff on imported solar panels after a big rise in imports. It says the tariff will protect jobs, but domestic firms that use solar panels complain about rising costs.
a Before the tariff in Extract A, one imported solar panel sold for L£285 in Lumera. Calculate its new price, assuming the whole tariff is passed on to buyers. Show your working. [2 mark(s)]
b With reference to Extract A, analyse the likely effects of the tariff on the market for solar panels in Lumera. [6 mark(s)]
c Evaluate whether the tariff on imported solar panels is likely to protect jobs in Lumera. Use Extract A and your own knowledge of economics to support your answer. [12 mark(s)]
