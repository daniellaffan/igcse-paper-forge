    • **Focus:** The kite pulling at the string 'as if it wanted to be somewhere else' becomes a symbol of the desire for freedom, perhaps the child's own.
    • **Descriptive skills:** The home-made kite is described with humorous detail: a bin bag, canes, sellotape and 'my mother's tights'.
    • **Descriptive skills:** The flight of the kite is described as an energetic sequence of running, burning string and climbing.

    • **Form and structure:** Repetition in 'and climbed, and climbed' imitates the kite rising.
