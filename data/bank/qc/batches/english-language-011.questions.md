# english-language — cold check batch english-language-011

Answer every question as a strong student would.

### english-language.w.review.trip  (45 marks)
 Your school magazine wants students to review recent school trips.

Write a review of a school trip or outing.

Your review may include:
• what you did and saw
• how worthwhile the trip was
• any other points you wish to make.

Your answer will be assessed on how accurately and appropriately you use vocabulary, spelling, punctuation and grammar. [45 mark(s)]
